🛠️ THE CORE CHALLENGE: SCALE

A national census requires capturing records for tens of millions of households and over 200 million citizens.

Flat-file or un-normalized architectures face catastrophic performance degradation and massive disk space bloating at this scale.

To design the schema of census database, we wanted to keep the db as optimized as possible. That's why we took some approaches that will scale up when we would have to store millions of data entries.

---

OUR APPROACH & OPTIMIZATION STRATEGIES

1. Achieving 3rd Normal Form (3NF) & Micro-Lookup Tables

To prevent referential integrity violations and massive data bloating, we advanced our schema strictly to 3NF.

Elimination of Redundant Text: Instead of repeating text strings like `"Tap Water"` or `"Employed Full-Time"` millions of times, we extracted these into micro-lookup tables (`Water_Source`, `Employment_Status`, `Religion`, `Gender`, etc.).

Compact Data Types: We linked these lookup tables using `TINYINT` or `SMALLINT` (1 or 2 bytes, up to 255 categories) instead of `INT` (4 bytes) or `VARCHAR` strings.
The Math: Saving 3 to 10+ bytes per column across dozens of columns over 200 million records prevents gigabytes of wasted storage, ensuring database blocks fit efficiently into system cache memory during heavy processing.

---

2. Hierarchical Chain & Strict Referential Integrity

Our schema maps the exact geographical and organizational hierarchy of Pakistan:

Province --> Division --> District --> Tehsil --> Union Council --> Census Block
--> Structure --> Household --> Person

To keep this deep relationship perfectly consistent without leaving orphaned data, we utilized declarative foreign keys with specific rules:

`ON DELETE CASCADE`: Used on operational tracking tables (e.g., deleting a profile automatically wipes their `Migration_History`).

`ON DELETE SET NULL`: Used on workforce management (e.g., if a supervisor in `Census_Staff` is removed, the field enumerator's data remains safe).

---

3. Query Optimization, Views, and Indexing

Massive multi-table joins can cripple performance. To combat this:

We wrapped complex multi-tier joins inside predefined logical SQL Views (e.g., `Most_Populated_Cities`, `Highly_Educated_Persons`, `Citizen_Record`).
This abstracts execution complexities and allows the database engine to optimize query compilation paths.
Indexing has been applied to high-frequency foreign keys to prevent full table scans.
